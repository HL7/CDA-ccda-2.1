Past Medical History (V3) [section, 2.16.840.1.113883.10.20.22.2.20, release 2015-08-01, open] view pdf View Updated C-CDA

Description:

This section contains a record of the patient's past complaints, problems, and diagnoses. It contains data from the patient's past up to the patient's current complaint or reason for seeking medical care.

Contained By: Contains:
Consultation Note
Discharge Summary
History and Physical
Transfer Summary
Referral Note
Procedure Note
Problem Observation
