Vital Sign Observation (V2) [observation, 2.16.840.1.113883.10.20.22.4.27, release 2014-06-09, open] view pdf View Updated C-CDA

Description:

This template represents measurement of common vital signs. Vital signs are represented with additional vocabulary constraints for type of vital sign and unit of measure.

The following is a list of recommended units for common types of vital sign measurements:

Name Unit
PulseOx %
Height/Head Circumf cm
Weight kg
Temp Cel
BP mm[Hg]
Pulse/Resp Rate /min
BMI kg/m2
BSA m2
inhaled oxygen concentration %
Contained By: Contains:
Vital Signs Organizer
Health Concern Act
Risk Concern Act
Author Participation

  • MAY contain zero or one [0..1] targetSiteCode (CONF:1098-7309).
